Meet your babysitter

Hello, I'm Judy Ombam

I am a Civil and Construction Engineering student at the University of Nairobi (Class of 2023), where I also serve as Secretary General in the Association of Civil Engineering Students (ACES).

Alongside my studies, I am deeply passionate about nurturing children in safe, warm, and positive environments. I have cared for children from newborns through teenagers, and across multiple cultural backgrounds — including Kenyan, American, Korean, and other international families — giving me a flexible understanding of different parenting styles.

I am also an active Sunday School teacher at church, which has significantly deepened my experience with children of all ages, temperaments, and learning styles. Through youth mentorship with the Umoja Foundation, I have further strengthened my ability to guide, support, and communicate with young people.

Questions answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "overnight" babysitting mean exactly? ▼
Overnight babysitting covers the hours from 7 PM to 7 AM — a full 12-hour period. I arrive at your home before 7 PM, settle your children through their bedtime routine and sleep, and remain present and awake as needed throughout the night until 7 AM the next morning.
Is National ID verification really required? ▼
Yes — this is a firm requirement for all bookings without exception. It protects both your children and me as the caregiver. Your National ID number and photo will be stored securely and never shared with any third parties. This is standard professional childcare practice.
Do you have experience with newborns? ▼
Yes. I have cared for newborns from 0–3 months old, including overnight bottle feeding, settling, and infant monitoring. Newborn/infant care is KSh 2,000 per night.
Can you handle children from different cultural backgrounds? ▼
Absolutely. I have experience caring for children from Kenyan, American, Korean, and other international families. I adapt to your family's cultural values, parenting style, dietary needs, and household routines — please include any relevant details in your booking notes.
Which areas do you serve? ▼
I serve Westlands, Parklands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Karen, and the Nairobi CBD. If your area is not listed, WhatsApp Judy to check — additional areas may be considered on request.
How does payment work? ▼
Payment is made on the day of service via M-Pesa only. For regular bookings, we can discuss a convenient arrangement to suit your schedule.
Do you have experience from Sunday School teaching? ▼
Yes — I am an active Sunday School teacher at my church. This experience has greatly expanded my ability to engage, teach, and care for children of all ages and temperaments in a structured, loving environment. It directly informs the patience and creativity I bring to overnight babysitting.
