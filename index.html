Meet your babysitter

Hello, I'm Beryl Ombam

I am a Civil and Construction Engineering student at the University of Nairobi (Class of 2023), where I also serve as Assistant Academic Secretary in the Association of Civil Engineering Students (ACES).

Alongside my studies, I am deeply passionate about nurturing children in safe, warm, and positive environments. I have cared for children from newborns through teenagers, and across multiple cultural backgrounds — including Kenyan, American, Korean, and other international families — giving me a flexible understanding of different parenting styles and child-rearing approaches.

I am also an active Sunday School teacher at church, which has significantly deepened my experience with children of all ages, temperaments, and learning styles. Teaching in that environment requires patience, creativity, and genuine love for children — all of which I bring directly into my overnight babysitting service.

Through youth mentorship with the Umoja Foundation, I have further strengthened my ability to guide, support, and communicate with young people. My engineering training has sharpened my problem-solving, organisation, and responsibility under pressure — qualities that directly translate into dependable overnight childcare.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages of children do you care for? ▼
I care for children across all ages — from newborns (0–3 months) right through to teenagers (up to 17 years). This includes infants, toddlers, school-age children, pre-teens, and teenagers. I have experience with every stage of childhood development.
Do you care for newborns and infants? ▼
Yes — infant and newborn care is one of my specialisations. I am experienced in bottle feeding, night settling, recognising different infant cries, and providing attentive overnight care for the youngest children. Infant overnight care starts from KSh 3,500.
Why do you require a National ID? ▼
For the safety of both your children and myself, I require a verified National ID from every parent or guardian before confirming an overnight booking. This is a standard requirement for professional childcare, helping ensure the security and trust of every arrangement. Your information is kept strictly confidential.
How far in advance should I book? ▼
At least 24–48 hours in advance is ideal, allowing time to confirm availability, review your children's routines, and complete the ID verification. That said, same-day emergency bookings are accepted (KSh 500 surcharge applies) — WhatsApp Beryl immediately.
Can you handle children from different cultural backgrounds? ▼
Absolutely. I have experience caring for children from Kenyan, American, Korean, and other international families. I adapt to your family's cultural values, parenting style, dietary needs, and household routines — please include any relevant details in your booking notes.
Which areas do you serve? ▼
I serve Westlands, Parklands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Karen, and the Nairobi CBD. If your area is not listed, WhatsApp Beryl to check — additional areas may be considered on request.
How does payment work? ▼
Payment is made on the day of service. I accept M-Pesa and cash. For regular bookings, we can discuss a convenient arrangement to suit your schedule.
Do you have experience from Sunday School teaching? ▼
Yes — I am an active Sunday School teacher at my church. This experience has greatly expanded my ability to engage, teach, and care for children of all ages and temperaments in a structured, loving environment. It directly informs the patience and creativity I bring to overnight babysitting.
